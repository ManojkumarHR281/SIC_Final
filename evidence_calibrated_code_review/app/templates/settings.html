Confidence methodology

Transparent and reproducible.

0.30 × rule reliability + 0.25 × evidence strength
+ 0.20 × detection certainty + 0.15 × context completeness
+ 0.10 × independent confirmation

Unavailable factors are omitted and remaining weights are renormalized. Sparse evidence applies a conservative completeness penalty. Verified syntax observations have a deterministic floor of 82% for the fact that the syntax is invalid; this does not imply runtime risk certainty.

Uncertainty

Residual doubt is combined with missing context, context-dependent behavior and detection certainty, then bounded to 0–100%. High confidence and low uncertainty are related but distinct claims.
